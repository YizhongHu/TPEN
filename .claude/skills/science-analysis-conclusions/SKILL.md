# Science analysis conclusions

**Read critically: the analyst may be wrong, miss evidence or misinterpret
it. Check an answer's evidence and reasoning before relying on it.**

After collection, a different agent writes the scientific analysis, unless
explicitly specified otherwise: different from the planner, pre-run
analyst, worker, orchestrator, manager and other earlier participants.
Write this experiment's own answers, with as much discussion as the
arguments need, rather than filling a form.

Exercise due diligence throughout. Instructions and prior accounts may be
incomplete; return to records, artifacts and code mid-analysis when needed.
Choose the calculations and checks relevant to your own analysis. Provide
evidence paths and hashes, pinned analysis code, and the commands or
calculations behind quantitative claims. These document your work; do not
prompt readers which evidence or reasoning to use. Readers choose their
own checks and reasoning. Say what was re-derived, by whom, and whether
anyone independently checked it; nobody yet is an honest account.

Hypothesize boldly but evaluate carefully. Explore alternative explanations
and unasked questions; one question can have several answers. Distinguish
observations, inferences and hypotheses. Seek evidence that could disprove
each answer; describe what was tried, what was found and what remains
unexamined. An unsupported idea can state what would test it. Bound answers
by the source, configuration, population and conditions examined; an absence
claim describes the search and its resolution. Discuss the evidence,
counter-evidence, assumptions and reasoning that matter, without grades or
an exhaustive numerical checklist.

**With each answer remind readers that you may have missed or misread
something, and that they must check its evidence and reasoning before
relying on it.**

Keep original records and distinguish later analysis methods from the
predeclared version. Expose missing evidence and its consequences. Request
extra preservation where needed, crucially including evidence that could
falsify your claims; a request does not itself authorize storage actions.

End with the warning below, without an overall conclusion or summary.

**Check the evidence and reasoning for yourself: the analyst may be wrong
or have overlooked something.**
